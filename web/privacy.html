MathPull Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: MathPull does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts and no analytics. The Android app can show one friendly reminder a day, and it does this entirely on your phone.

Who we are

MathPull (Android package com.farooqbuilds.mathpull) is a maths tug-of-war game for two players or one player against a computer bot. It is developed by Syed Farooq Israr (FarooqBuilds).

What information we collect

None. MathPull does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, age, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or any other third-party tracking service.

Daily reminder (Android)

The Android app can remind you to play with one friendly notification a day. The reminder is on by default at 5:00 PM. When you open MathPull for the first time on Android 13 or newer, Android asks whether MathPull may show notifications. If you say no, no reminders are shown.

The reminder is created and shown by your phone itself. It does not use the internet, a server or an account. It is shown at most once a day, only between 8 AM and 8 PM, it is skipped on a day when you already played, and it stops by itself if you do not open the app for a week. You can turn it off at any time with the bell button on the start screen, or in your phone’s notification settings.

What stays on your device

MathPull remembers your sound and music choices, whether the daily reminder is on and at what time, and on which days you finished a game (the days are used to show your week in the Daily Pull row and are kept for 60 days). These small settings are saved on your own device (in the Android app, in the app’s private storage; in the web version, in your browser’s local storage). They are never sent anywhere. Game scores and questions are used only while a match is running and are not saved.

Permissions (Android)

The Android app uses the permissions that notifications need: to show them, to bring the reminder back after the phone restarts, and for the standard notification vibration. It does not ask for the internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Internet access

The Android app works fully offline and does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data to us or anyone else. The optional web version is delivered by our hosting provider (Cloudflare). Like any website, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages. We do not receive or use that data for any other purpose. The web version does not show reminders.

Children

MathPull is designed to be suitable for everyone, including children aged 6 and over. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. The daily reminder is gentle: one a day, never at night, with no pressure messages, and it can be turned off with one tap. The app contains no ads, no links that lead out of the app, no chat and no social features.

Third parties and sharing

We do not share, sell or disclose any information to third parties, because we do not collect any.

Fonts and audio

The fonts (Fredoka and Nunito) and all music and sound effects are bundled inside the app. Nothing is downloaded while you play.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
